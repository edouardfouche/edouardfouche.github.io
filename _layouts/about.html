Short Biography

Edouard Fouché is a French-German AI engineer and data scientist with a product-building mindset. He leads AI-driven business transformation, which entails (1) identifying business processes where AI creates measurable value and building the software that delivers it, and (2) empowering the workforce to adopt new ways of working with AI.

Since February 2026, Edouard has worked as a Senior Key Expert for Data Science & AI at Siemens Healthineers in Erlangen, Germany, where he brings AI to strategic procurement: spend analysis, negotiation support, risk quantification and supplier segmentation. He joined Siemens AG in 2023 as a Data Scientist for Product Innovation, where he was the tech lead of large-scale AI forecasting software. He has filed more than six invention disclosures and regularly gives keynotes to executive audiences on turning AI into business value.

Before moving to industry, Edouard spent seven years at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT). He earned his Ph.D. in Data Science Summa Cum Laude in 2020 and received the Helmholtz Doctoral Prize for his work on extracting knowledge from large data streams. He then led a research group and was the principal lecturer for Data Science for three years. He has published more than 20 research papers at venues such as KDD, ICDM, ECML and JMLR, and did research stays at the University of Illinois (2019) and Kyoto University (2022). He studied engineering and computer science at ESIEE Paris and KIT, and holds an MBA from the Quantic School of Business and Technology.

On this blog, he writes about how AI is changing software engineering and knowledge work, and why it makes human judgment more important, not less.
