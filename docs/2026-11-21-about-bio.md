# About page: new biography

**Date:** 2026-11-21
**Scope:** the text of the About page (`/about/`) and the meta descriptions that describe the site
owner's role. No design or layout changes.

## Observations

- The biography in [`_layouts/about.html`](../_layouts/about.html) stopped in October 2023. It
  described the owner as working on "decision-making problems in energy systems", "on a mission to make
  the energy grid better, faster, stronger", with the last position at Siemens Smart Infrastructure.
- Newer sources disagree with it:
  - The downloadable CV ([`Resume_CV_FOUCHE.pdf`](../Resume_CV_FOUCHE.pdf), last updated in April
    2026) lists Siemens Healthineers since February 2026, working on AI for strategic procurement, and
    the 2025 nomination as Senior Key Expert in Data Science.
  - The public LinkedIn profile (only partly visible without logging in) lists Siemens Healthineers as
    the current company, and its headline begins "Full-stack AI-Engineer with a Product-building
    mindset".
  - The 2026 blog posts are about AI in software engineering and knowledge work, not energy systems.
- The same outdated description was in the About page's `description:` front matter, which
  jekyll-seo-tag uses for the meta description, Open Graph, Twitter card and JSON-LD (see
  [SEO](2026-11-20-seo.md)).
- The site-wide `description` in [`_config.yml`](../_config.yml) (home page meta description and RSS
  feed description) and the CV page's description said only "data scientist".

## Changes

| File | Change |
| --- | --- |
| [`_layouts/about.html`](../_layouts/about.html) | New biography in four paragraphs: positioning (AI-driven business transformation), current role at Siemens Healthineers and the Siemens history, the KIT years and education, and what the blog is about. The funding bodies (DFG, BW-Stiftung, GSO) were dropped. |
| [`_layouts/about.html`](../_layouts/about.html) | "Miscellaneous" list: "preferred programming languages are Python and Scala" became "I mostly work in Python, and these days I review more AI-written code than I write myself". "Available for (non-)technical talks and consulting gigs" now links to Tengu Consulting (`/services/`). |
| [`about.md`](../about.md) | Meta description: "a French-German AI engineer and data scientist with a Ph.D. from KIT, focused on AI-driven business transformation" (150 characters). |
| [`_config.yml`](../_config.yml) | Site description: "Blog of Edouard Fouché, French-German AI engineer and data scientist. Articles on AI, software engineering, research and careers, plus CV and publications." (155 characters, the limit noted in the file). "Machine learning" became "software engineering" to match the recent posts. |
| [`cv.md`](../cv.md) | Meta description: "data scientist" became "AI engineer and data scientist". |

The biography text was written by the site owner. On review, "He is a thought leader in AI-driven
business transformation" became "He leads AI-driven business transformation": a self-applied
"thought leader" label tends to be discounted by readers, while "leads" states the same role
directly. Otherwise only copy edits were applied: tense ("Since February 2026, Edouard has worked"),
"empowering the workforce to adopt",
"tech lead of large-scale AI forecasting software" (uncountable "software"), a lowercase generic "data
scientist", the venue list "KDD, ICDM, ECML and JMLR", and `&amp;` for the ampersand in the HTML.

Not changed: the hobbies line ("cooking, hiking, photography, and roller derby"), pending a decision by
the owner.

## Verification

- `bundle exec jekyll build` succeeds.
- In `_site/about/index.html`, the meta description, `og:description` and the JSON-LD `description`
  carry the new text, and the four paragraphs and the edited list render as intended.
- `_site/index.html` and `_site/feed.xml` carry the new site description, and `_site/cv/index.html`
  the new CV description.
- `.profilpic` floats right and `.content-paragraph` sets no `clear` (in
  [`css/customstyle.css`](../css/customstyle.css)), so all four paragraphs wrap around the portrait as
  the single paragraph did. On narrow screens the portrait is not floated and sits above the text, as
  before.
- A search of `_layouts/`, `_includes/`, `_config.yml` and the top-level stubs for "grid", "Smart
  Infrastructure", "energy system" and "data scientist" finds no other outdated role description. The
  publication list and the posts are records and were not searched for changes.
